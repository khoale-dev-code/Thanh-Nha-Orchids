# Thanh Nha invoice V23.3.1

- Uses the existing receipt item line_subtotal; no recalculation of money.
- No ordinal numbers before product names.
- Name, quantity, line total on separate lines, readable on 80mm paper.
- Keeps carton sales, carton price per carton, VietQR, image export and Android sharing.
- Suppresses generic 'Mặc định' variant label, preserves meaningful variants.
- In this UI, 'Giá' means the extended amount for that product line (quantity times its unit price), not per-unit price.

Test on 80mm printer and on the PNG share function before live use.
